The Nutshell Games are a free, and open to the public competition put on by the [Virginia Tech Center for Communicating Science](https://communicatingscience.isce.vt.edu/). 30 PhD students presented their research in 90 seconds or less in front of a crowd on the main stage at the Moss Arts Center. My talk was on fly poison, a native Appalachian wildflower that apparently is loved by invasive mosquitoes!
